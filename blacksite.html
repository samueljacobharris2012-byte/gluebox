VR: left stick move (push all the way), right stick turn. Grip = grab, trigger = shoot / pull grenade pin. Your AK stays slung on your chest. Push the red button by the base door to open it. A/X drops the mag; reach to your LEFT POCKET (left thigh) and grip for a new mag, push it in, grab the charging handle and pull it back. Hold the foregrip with your other hand. Knife on the left of your chest, grenades on the right of your chest. DESERT EAGLE in the holster on your right hip; its mags are in your RIGHT POCKET. B/Y = menu.
PC: WASD, mouse aim, click shoot, 1/2 or Q or mouse wheel to swap AK and Deagle, right click aim down the scope, R reload, G grenade, F knife, E use, Shift sprint, Esc menu.
