Gluebox info

A first-person physics sandbox where you glue parts together to build cars, planes, boats, contraptions and whatever else you can think of.

Quick start

Build your first car in about a minute.

  1. Open gluebox.net and click Play. The game needs a keyboard and mouse. Press Esc any time to free your mouse.
  2. Press B to open the build menu and pick a Plywood Panel. Right-click (RMB) to spawn it.
  3. Spawn a Driver Seat, hold it against the panel with LMB, and press G to glue it on.
  4. Glue four Motor Wheels to the sides. A wheel glued to a body turns into a motorized axle.
  5. Walk up to the seat and press E to sit. W/S drive and A/D steer.
  6. Too lazy to build? The build menu has ready-made vehicles like the Starter Car and Starter Plane.

About the game

What Gluebox is and who made it.

  • Gluebox is a sandbox game. There is no right way to play. Build stuff, wreck stuff, mess around.
  • Everything runs on real physics. Glue joints can snap in big crashes and explosions.
  • Play solo, or play multiplayer on public servers or private servers locked with a code. Multiplayer has proximity voice chat.
  • There is a day and night cycle. At night Husks rise out of the ground and crumble at sunrise.
  • You can make your own maps with the Map Creator and record stunts with the Clip Editor (F8 turns the last 5 minutes into a video).
  • Gluebox runs right in your browser. Nothing to download.

Items

Things you hold in your hand. Press Q to switch between them.

Tools

  • Physics Gun: grab anything from far away. Scroll to push or pull it, RMB freezes it in mid-air.
  • Grappling Hook: hook anything up to 95 m away and reel in. Light stuff flies to you, heavy stuff pulls you.
  • Duplicator: right-click a build to copy it, left-click the ground to paste it.
  • Material Tool: turn a part into stone, steel, gold, glass, ice, rubber and more.
  • Size Ray: make things bigger or smaller.
  • Wiring Tool: wire buttons and logic parts together.
  • Paintbrush: draw on anything. Scroll picks the color.
  • Spray Can: spray an image from your computer onto any surface.

Weapons

  • You start with a Pistol, Shotgun, SMG, Rocket Launcher and Grenade Launcher.
  • Nerf Blaster: super fast, does almost no damage, pushes things around.
  • Sniper Rifle: long range, big hits.
  • Thunder Staff: a lightning gun that chains between enemies. You get it by beating the Husk Titan.
  • Crafted weapons: Stone Hammer, Spear, Stone Axe and Crystal Sword (see Crafting below).
  • Switch to your fists to grab things again.

Objects

Parts you spawn from the build menu (B) and glue together.

Vehicle parts

  • Motor Wheel: spins when you glue it onto a vehicle with a seat.
  • Spring Wheel: bouncy wheel with crazy grip. It can climb mountains.
  • Sticky Wheel: drives on walls. Try it at Sticky Park.
  • Driver Seat: sit in it with E to drive whatever it's glued to.
  • Thruster: E turns it on. Pushes away from the nozzle.
  • Wing, Tail Wing, Tail Fin and Propeller Engine: glue them to a body with a seat to make a plane.
  • Helicopter Rotor: glue it on top of anything with a seat and it lifts off.
  • Pontoon and Boat Propeller: build boats.
  • Ski: slides with almost no friction. Put two under a build with a thruster.
  • Stop Block: stops the whole vehicle dead.

Moving parts

  • Hinge: glue the steel side to the base and the brass side to the part that swings.
  • Motor Hinge: same thing, but E swings it 90 degrees.
  • Piston: E pushes it out 1.5 m.
  • Hoverball: floats. Space and Shift move it up and down while driving.
  • Gyro Stabilizer: keeps your build from flipping over.
  • Spinner: spins whatever is glued to it.
  • Balloon: ties on with a rope and lifts about 7 kg.
  • Release Block: vanishes when you press R, dropping whatever hangs from it.

Boom

  • TNT: E lights a 3 second fuse. Hard crashes set it off too.
  • Mini Nuke: E arms a 5 second fuse. Get far away.
  • Firework: E launches it.
  • Turrets like the Shotgun Turret and Sniper Turret aim at whatever your crosshair is on.

Weird science

  • Gravity Well: E turns on a floating black hole.
  • Tesla Coil: zaps Husks.
  • Teleporter Pad: place two or more. Step on one and you pop out of the next.
  • Beat Box: program drums and synths on a 16-step grid. Everyone nearby hears it.
  • Parachute Pack: E opens it.
  • Torch: lights up the dark. Glue it to a wall or stick it in the ground.

Crafting

Gather stuff, then make weapons at the Workbench.

  • Press F on trees to get Sticks. Press F on boulders to get Stone and Iron Ore.
  • Press E on a material to stash it. Press I to see what you have.
  • Glue materials onto the Workbench, then press E at the bench.
  • Stick + Stone = Stone Hammer
  • 2 Sticks + Stone = Spear
  • Stick + 2 Stones = Stone Axe
  • Stick + Crystal = Crystal Sword

Enemies

Things that want to get you.

  • Husks rise out of the ground at night and crumble at sunrise. Set the world to Always day if you just want to build.
  • Husk Titan: a giant husk that shows up every night. It slams the ground and throws boulders. Beat it for the Thunder Staff.
  • Zombies come in waves in Zombie Town. Every wave is tougher than the last.
  • Storms roll in sometimes. Lightning hits the tallest metal part nearby.

Tips

  • Wood floats, steel and stone sink. Rafts actually work.
  • Look at a Thruster, gun or lamp and press K to give it its own key.
  • To glue from far away, press G on part A, then G on part B.
  • To break one glue joint, look at the amber glue blob and press Z.
  • Press F5 before you try something dumb, then F9 to undo it.
