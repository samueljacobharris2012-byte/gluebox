Controls

Keyboard: WASD move, Space jump, Shift dash, F or click to swing the bat / shoot the gun, Esc pause. 2nd keyboard player: Arrows, Enter jump, R-Ctrl dash, / hit.
Controller: left stick move, bottom button jump, right button or bumpers dash, left button or triggers hit, Start pause.
Touch: drag the left side to move, buttons on the right.
Tips: explosions crack and break the floor, and broken tiles grow back after a while. Jump over shockwaves, bullets and waves. Dashing makes you invincible for a split second.
