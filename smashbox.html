SMASHBOX

Smash everything. Grab weapons and ammo from your backpack. Guns are reloaded by hand.

VR: left stick walk (push all the way), right stick turn, grip grab, trigger shoot / hold trigger + grip to grab from far, A/X drop magazine, B/Y menu, click and hold a thumbstick for slow-mo.
Phone: left side move, right side look. PC: click to aim with mouse, WASD, E grab/use, left click shoot or swing, Q mag out, R rack, T load, G insert, B backpack, hold Z or Shift for slow-mo, right mouse aim, M mods, Esc menu.
Grenades: hold one, pull the pin, throw. In VR, grab the pin ring with your other hand. Attachments: in VR bring a scope, laser, etc. to the gun and it clicks on (grab it with your other hand to pull it off). Melee mods: touch them to the weapon.
