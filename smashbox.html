SMASHBOX

Smash everything. Grab weapons and ammo from your backpack. Guns are reloaded by hand.

VR: left stick walk (push all the way), right stick turn, grip grab, trigger shoot / hold trigger + grip to grab from far, A/X drop magazine, B/Y menu, click and hold a thumbstick for slow-mo.
Phone: left side move, right side look. PC: click to aim with mouse, WASD, E grab/use, left click shoot or swing, Q mag out, R rack, T load, G insert, B backpack, hold Z or Shift for slow-mo, Esc menu.
