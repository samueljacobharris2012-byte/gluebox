Get into the game

Open Gluebox and find your way around the menus.

  • Go to gluebox.net and click Play.
  • Gluebox needs a keyboard and mouse. Clicking Play locks your mouse so you can look around.
  • Pick a map. Valley is a good one to start on: lots of flat grass to build on.
  • Press Esc to free your mouse and pause. Press Tab to open the pause menu, or H for help.
Tip: If you just want to build without enemies, set the clock to Always day. Then the night Husks never show up.

Move around

Get used to walking, jumping and flying.

  • WASD to walk, Space to jump, hold Shift to sprint.
  • Hold C to crouch. You get shorter and slower, so you can sneak under things.
  • Press V to turn on the jetpack. Hold Space to fly. Hold Shift while falling to land softer. Fuel refills on the ground.
  • Press J to go ragdoll. Press J again to stand back up.
  • Press L for slow motion. Press it again for normal speed.

Spawn and grab stuff

Learn how to get parts out and move them around.

Pick a part

  • Press B to open the build menu. It shows every part in the game.
  • Click a part to put it on your selected hotbar slot.
  • Use 1 to 0 or the scroll wheel to pick a slot.

Spawn it

  • Right-click (RMB) to spawn the part in front of you. Try spawning a Plywood Panel.

Grab it

  • Hold left-click (LMB) on a part to pick it up and carry it.
  • While holding it, hold R and move your mouse to rotate it. Tap R or T to snap it to a clean angle.
  • Right-click while holding something to throw it.
  • Press Z on a part to delete it.

4

Glue things together

Glue is the whole game. Here is how it works.

  • Pick up a part, hold it against another part, and press G. Now they're stuck together.
  • Hold a part against the ground and press G to anchor it in place.
  • Gluing from far away: without holding anything, press G on part A, then G on part B.
  • Press X to unglue a part.
  • Every glue joint shows up as an amber blob. Look at one and press Z to break just that joint.
  • Glue isn't unbreakable. Big crashes and explosions snap joints.
Try it: Glue three Plywood Panels into a big U shape. Pick it up. The whole thing moves together.

Build your first car

Make something you can drive.

  1. Spawn a Plywood Panel. This is the body of your car.
  2. Spawn a Driver Seat, hold it on top of the panel, and press G.
  3. Spawn a Motor Wheel and glue it to one side of the panel. A wheel glued to a body turns into a motorized axle.
  4. Glue three more Motor Wheels so there's one on each corner.
  5. Walk up to the seat and press E to sit down.
  6. W/S to drive forward and back, A/D to steer. Press E again to get out.

Upgrades

  • Glue a Thruster on the back. Space fires it while you drive.
  • Swap in Spring Wheels. They have so much grip they can climb mountains.
  • Glue on a Stop Block and press E on it to stop the car dead.
Shortcut: The build menu has a ready-made Starter Car if you just want to drive.

Make it fly

Planes, helicopters and hover cars.

Plane

  • Glue Wings, a Tail Wing, a Tail Fin and a Propeller Engine to a body with a Driver Seat.
  • W is throttle, Space/Shift tilt the nose up and down, A/D turn.
  • Or grab the ready-made Starter Plane from the build menu.

Helicopter

  • Glue a Helicopter Rotor on top of anything with a seat. Sit down and it lifts off.
  • Space/Shift climb and sink, W/S fly, A/D turn.

Hover car

  • Glue Hoverballs under a build. While driving, Space moves them up and Shift moves them down.
  • Add a Gyro Stabilizer so it doesn't flip over.

Make things move

Hinges, pistons and your own keybinds.

  • Hinge: glue the steel side to the base and the brass side to the part that should swing.
  • Motor Hinge: same thing, but E swings it 90 degrees.
  • Piston: glue the base and the red head to different parts. E pushes it out 1.5 m.
  • E also turns on hoverballs, spinners, fans, turrets, lamps and thrusters.
  • Keybinds: look at a part, press K, then press any key. Now that key runs it, even while you're driving.
  • Rope: pick the rope with =, then right-click one spot and then another to tie them together.
Try it: Build a door with a Motor Hinge and bind it to O.

Use your items

Tools and weapons you hold in your hand.

  • Press Q to switch items. LMB or F uses or fires them. Switch back to fists to grab things again.
  • You start with a Pistol, Shotgun, SMG, Rocket Launcher and Grenade Launcher.
  • Physics Gun: grab anything from far away. Scroll to push or pull, RMB freezes it in mid-air.
  • Grappling Hook: hook onto anything up to 95 m away and reel in. Space lets go with a boost.
  • Duplicator: right-click a build to copy it, left-click the ground to paste it.
  • Material Tool: turn parts into stone, steel, gold, glass, ice, rubber and more. Ice slides, rubber bounces, metal sticks to magnets.
  • Paintbrush: draw on anything. Scroll picks the color.

Survive the night

Enemies, gathering and crafting.

Enemies

  • At night, Husks rise out of the ground. They crumble at sunrise.
  • A giant Husk Titan shows up every night. Beat it to get the Thunder Staff.
  • Glue Torches to walls or stick them in the ground to light up the dark.

Gather and craft

  • Press F on trees to get Sticks. Press F on boulders to get Stone and Iron Ore.
  • Press E on a material to stash it. Press I to see what you have.
  • Glue materials onto the Workbench, then press E at the bench:
  • Stick + Stone = Stone Hammer
  • 2 Sticks + Stone = Spear
  • Stick + 2 Stones = Stone Axe
  • Stick + Crystal = Crystal Sword

Save, record and play with friends

Keep your stuff and show it off.

  • Press F5 to save a snapshot and F9 to load it. Save before you try something dumb.
  • Your world saves, so you can hit Continue next time.
  • Press F8 to open the Clip Editor. It turns the last 5 minutes into a video with your own camera angles, effects and music.
  • Play multiplayer on a public server, or make a private one locked with a code for your friends.
  • Turn on voice chat in the Multiplayer screen. Hold N to talk. People sound louder the closer they are.
