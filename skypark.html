SKYPARK
A trampoline park in your pocket

Flip Zone

Hold a side to flip, let go to land feet first. Hold both sides to side launch out of a bad landing. Turn on Tilt in the game to spin by tilting your phone.

Dodgeball

Pull back and let go to throw. Tap to hop over balls. Tap right as a ball reaches you to catch it and knock the thrower out.

Sky Climb

Bounce up an endless tower of floating trampolines. Tilt your phone to steer and wrap around the edges. Tap to flip, but don't land halfway through one. Orange pads launch you, foam pads break.

Slam Dunk

Tilt your phone to move. Tap as you land to pump higher, tap in the air to flip, and tap when your hands are in the green zone to dunk. Flip before a dunk for bonus points.
